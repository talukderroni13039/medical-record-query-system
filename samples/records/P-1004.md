## Chief Complaint
Sneezing, blocked nose, and itchy eyes every spring.

Symptoms peak in April and May and are worse outdoors. Also has itchy dry patches on the inside of both elbows. Sleep is disturbed by nasal congestion.

## Past Medical History
Eczema since childhood. No asthma.

## Medications

- Hydrocortisone 1% cream twice daily to elbows

## Allergies
Cat dander (sneezing and itchy eyes).

## Lab Results
- Total IgE 410 IU/mL
- Skin prick test positive for grass pollen and cat dander

## Assessment
Seasonal allergic rhinitis with atopic eczema.

## Plan
Add fluticasone nasal spray daily during pollen season. Emollients for eczema. Consider immunotherapy referral if not controlled.
